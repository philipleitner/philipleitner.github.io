# Issuer logo sources

Brand assets identify the issuers of the listed credentials; trademarks remain with their respective owners.

- **Bloomberg:** original PNG from Bloomberg's official public website repository, [bloomberg/bloomberg.github.io](https://github.com/bloomberg/bloomberg.github.io/blob/48e104b08c781d0f1865c8adbe7f21462d43709c/jsnation-2022-record-tuple/media/bloomberg.png). Saved unchanged. CSS displays the original upper Bloomberg wordmark and excludes the lower Engineering lockup without distorting or redrawing the image.
- **Bank of America:** existing red-and-blue SVG wordmark from [detain/svg-logos](https://github.com/detain/svg-logos/blob/b02ee1ac30c7ff4757278337c95588b01ed0954b/svg/b/bank-of-america-logo.svg). Saved unchanged; original colours #012169 and #E31837 retained.

Downloaded 4 November 2026. Both assets are served locally; no runtime third-party image requests. Issuer wordmarks have meaningful alt text; the duplicate issuer-name pills were removed from the visible entries.
