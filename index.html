01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

Growing up in five different countries made me curious about everyday differences: why going out for dinner costs more in one place than another and why so many people in Singapore had their groceries delivered, while in Austria that wasn’t even an option.

Wanting to understand these differences drew me towards economics, but it also raised questions about businesses themselves. Why had a company seen an opportunity in one country and not another? How would it decide whether expanding was worth the investment? Those questions brought me to finance.

During my time at UWCSEA, that interest grew into a habit of following financial news and paying closer attention to markets. I’ve since kept building on this interest through reading, projects and countless other things. What I enjoy most is how people can look at the same company or event and reach completely different conclusions. Trying to understand those perspectives, form my own view and rethink it when new information comes along is what keeps me interested.
